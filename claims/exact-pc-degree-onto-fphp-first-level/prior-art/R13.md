Claims covered: run 20261027-0607-proof-complexity K13, K14, K15 (exact PC degree p for onto-FPHP^{n+pc}_n over Z/p, p not dividing c, n >= 2p-3; the cross-characteristic separation); run 20261028-0841-proof-complexity K17, K18 (no degree-4 PC refutation over Z/2 for k = 8, n >= 7; eventually for every k = 2^l, l >= 3); run 20261102-1249-proof-complexity K1-K6 (no PC refutation of line degree <= p over Z/p for k = c p^2, n >= 2p-1, every prime p).

Why the earlier "none" verdicts (0607 R12, 0841 R8) are replaced by "partial": the second pass of the literature ledger (2026-10-29), after those verdicts were recorded, opened Riis-Sitharam ECCC TR97-048. Its Theorem 33 is a polynomial-calculus degree lower bound for the same axiom family (onto-PHP^{N+p^l}_N over characteristic p): degree >= 2^l - 1 once N >= (21+3p^2)(2^l - 2) - p. That is a direct overlap on the prime-power surplus family. Our bounds differ in size and threshold (details under Related work); for k = 8 at large N, Theorem 33 as stated is stronger than K18 (>= 7 against >= 5), and our contribution there is the small-n range 7 <= n < 196 and the machine-checked proof. Theorem 33's proof status is unresolved in our ledger: we have not checked it, and we make no priority claim either way. At l = 1 (K13-K15), Theorem 33 gives only degree >= 1, and the surplus k = pc with c > 1 not a power of p is outside its family; we still record the verdict as partial because the family and the question are the ones Riis-Sitharam and Beame-Riis studied, and the upper bound is Riis's.

Conventions: functional encoding of onto-FPHP; PC degree is line degree (maximal degree of a line), as in the Lean library. The Beame-Riis Nullstellensatz degree is multiplier degree, one less than line degree.
