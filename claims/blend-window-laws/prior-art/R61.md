# Prior-art answer for R17 (K28: mutual cooperation with F_k iff cooperation at every height; DefectBot blends never reach it)

Verdict: partial.

- The tool behind it is known: Barasz et al. (arXiv 1401.5577, Section 4 and the Kripke-frame footnote in Section 5) evaluate modal agents on linear GL frames and read the outcome in the standard model off the eventually constant value at large heights; that Box^k p at height h means "true at all heights below h-k+1" is the standard semantics. Their WaitFairBot_K, which defects at low heights, is an example of an agent that therefore fails to cooperate with FairBot. Not found: the general statement that ending in CC with F_k is equivalent to cooperating at every height, or the resulting exclusion for every DefectBot blend.
- Write-up: present it as a short lemma that follows from the standard height semantics (cite Barasz et al. and Boolos, The Logic of Provability), then the exclusion as its consequence; group with K13 and K27.
