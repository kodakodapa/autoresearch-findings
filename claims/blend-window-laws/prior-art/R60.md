# Owner prior-art check for K9 (DefectBot and CooperateBot height blends of W_n)

Verdict: partial.

- Guarding an agent so that it defects at low heights is the construction of WaitFairBot_K (Barasz et al., Section 5), applied there to FairBot; K9's DefectBot blends apply it to W_n with arbitrary height sets. Not found: the self-cooperation criterion for these blends or the CooperateBot side. Same situation as K8 (R4).
