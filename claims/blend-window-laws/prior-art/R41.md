# Prior-art answer for R6 (K14: graded FairBot height blends of W_n cooperate with themselves iff S meets {k,...,n-1})

Verdict: none found.

- No source states this law or studies deterministic blends between self-defecting and self-cooperating modal agents. The nearest known construction is the height guard not Box^K False of WaitFairBot_K (Barasz et al., arXiv 1401.5577, Section 5), which switches between DefectBot and FairBot behaviour by height; it is not a blend with a self-defecting agent and has no self-play law of this kind. Box^k p (the F_k here) are known as unexploitable self-cooperators (LessWrong 2026); W_n is our own earlier finding.
