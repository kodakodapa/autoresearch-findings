# Prior-art answer for R7 (K12: round robin, Nash and ESS up to size 9)

Verdict: none found.
- No source runs a round robin, a symmetric Nash analysis or an ESS analysis over a census of modal agents. Garrabrant (Costs to Modeling) analyses equilibria among four agents (CB, DB, FB, PB) with modelling costs; Mennen studies Nash equilibria of modal combat in other games. Neither enumerates agents.
- Evolutionary simulations exist for decision-theory agents (e.g. FDT vs CDT, arXiv 2005.05154) but not for modal agents.
