# Prior-art answer for R5 (K9: W_n unexploitable, cooperates with FairBot and CooperateBot, defects against itself)

Verdict: none found.

- No source shows a modal agent with this combination (see also R3). The delay technique with nested boxes is known from WaitFairBot_K (Barasz et al. 2014, obstacles section), which is the nearest relative; the W_n gate and its properties were not found.
- Barasz et al. discuss unexploitability for FairBot, PrudentBot and CliqueBot, all of which cooperate with themselves.
