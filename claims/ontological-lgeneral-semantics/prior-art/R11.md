K17 makes three claims. (i) In K4's HOL, a proof system that only requires the instances of ∀E/∃I at types of L to be L-terms (the "witness" criterion) but allows unrestricted Leibniz substitution derives A∃p.(Atom p ∧ p) from any theory. (ii) That sentence fails in some v4 L-general model *of variant 2*. (iii) Adding the motive clause restores soundness. The closest prior source is Benzmüller's own arXiv 2609.36279 v4 (2026-10-14), which the arXiv page lists as the latest version. It covers (iii) and the ingredients of (i) and (ii), and it explicitly lists the rest as open:

- (iii) is essentially `sources/2609.36279/v4/main.tex`, Proposition "soundness over L-general models" (`prop:lgeneral`), together with Definition `def:lgeneral`. Its hypothesis covers every L-typed position "wherever in π they lie --- in a hypothesis, a motive, a side of an equation". The proof treats Leibniz instances whose parameters lie outside L as forbidden by that hypothesis. So soundness *with* a motive/whole-term clause is already stated and proved there. K17's version would be a restatement or refinement of it.
- (i) and (ii): Example `ex:separating` (same file, Section 8.3, "What the criterion establishes…") gives the same sentence φ = A∃p.(Atom p ∧ p). It shows that the embedding proves φ with the nominal λu. u = w as the witness, and it builds a Fraenkel–Mostowski / finitely-supported L-general model with no atoms in which φ fails. But that model is a model of the *empty* theory only. The text says explicitly that M "is not a model of the axioms of variant 2", because modal collapse holds there. The paper also states that the witness criterion alone does not yield `prop:lgeneral`, and its example is a formula introduced by excluded middle, not a Leibniz motive. It leaves two questions open: whether "a nominal can never hide in a motive alone" for the article's axioms, and "whether a separating model exists for any of them". See the open-questions passage just before `\section{Verification}` (`sec:verification`), which points to Section `sec:criterion-open`.

So the specific new content of K17 has no published counterpart in this search. That content is a concrete derivation of φ in which the nominal sits only in a Leibniz motive, while every ∀E/∃I instance is an L-term, together with an L-general model *of variant 2* refuting φ. It would answer the open questions of 2609.36279 v4 negatively for variant 2. The companion paper 2609.26806 v3 (`sources/2609.26806/v3/main.tex`, `lean.tex`) has no L-general semantics. Its Leibniz-equality material is only the embedding's primitive equality.

Outside Benzmüller's work, related ideas are known. Leibniz equality can fail to be identity in sparse Henkin/general models (Andrews 1972, as discussed in arXiv 2602.20038). General frames for propositional quantifiers can be atomless, and the atomicity principle holds on Kripke frames but not on general frames (Ding & Li, arXiv 2406.16034). With propositional quantification, nominals are definable over full frames (ten Cate 2006, cited in v4). None of these addresses the witness-versus-motive criterion or variant 2.
