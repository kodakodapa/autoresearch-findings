The closest prior art is Example `ex:separating` in Benzmüller, "Proofs Without Nominals" (sources/2609.36279/v4/main.tex, Section "What the criterion establishes, and what it leaves open" (sec:criterion-open), Definition `def:lgeneral`, Proposition `prop:lgeneral`, Example `ex:separating`; Lean file anc/OpenQuestions/lean/Separation.lean, theorems `no_atom`, `everyWorldAnAtom_fails`, `everyWorldAnAtom_standard`). The paper defines the same sentence A ∃p.(Atom p ∧ p), using the difference modality D and its own `Atom` definition. It builds a model with W = infinite 0-1 sequences, one individual, finitely supported (finitary) carriers from a Fraenkel–Mostowski/Pitts construction and no atoms. The sentence then fails at every world, although it holds in every standard model, and the HOL embedding proves it with a nominal. That covers the model-theoretic core of K7: W = 2^N, one entity, an L-closed general model without atoms, and failure of the atom sentence everywhere, which no proof meeting the whole-term condition can derive.

But the paper's model uses the **total** relation and **empty** positivity. The paper says explicitly that this model is not a model of variant 2, whatever positivity is chosen: MC holds hybrid-free, and under the total relation it fails for finitary sets other than ∅ and W. Section `sec:open` then leaves open "whether a separating model exists for any of them [the axioms]". K7 does three things the paper does not. It switches to the identity frame, where □X = X and MC holds trivially. It takes positivity to be truth of the property of the one entity (Pφw = φ()w). It uses closed, eqT-respecting carriers. With these, K7 gets a model of variant 2 (Ax1Gen, Ax2a, Ax2b, Ax3, Ax4) plus MC that separates. So K7 answers, for variant 2, a question the source states as open. I found no published model of this kind for variant 2 or any other variant. The Lean 4 / THF port (2609.26806) contains no such model; a grep of its v3 main.tex and the anc/ files found no separating-model construction.

Atomless general models for propositionally quantified modal logic are known in general (the atomless principle under bisimulation-quantifier readings; atomic general frames in completeness proofs; ten Cate on propositional quantifiers versus hybrid ↓/@). None of this work involves the ontological axioms. "None found" for the variant-2 model means only that this search did not find one.

## Related work

Benzmüller, "Proofs Without Nominals …", https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36279 (v4), Example ex:separating: an atomless L-general model on 2^N (total frame, empty positivity, one individual) in which A∃p.(Atom p ∧ p) fails everywhere, formalised in Isabelle and Lean (propositional part).
