All four claims (K1–K4) are about truth in $\mathcal L$-general models ("ValidV4"). The closest prior art is Benzmüller's "Proofs Without Nominals" (arXiv 2609.36279, v4, the latest in `sources/`). That paper proves weaker or neighbouring results for each claim, and it lists the exact statements K1–K4 make as **open**. This search found no published work, on arXiv or elsewhere, that settles any of them.

**K1 (Pos G from the rigid instance at PosGlobal, with Ax2a, Ax2b, Ax4, over v4 general models).**
- Source: `sources/2609.36279/v4/main.tex`, §6.4.3 "Ax1GenR: rigid sets" (`sec:rigid`).
- What the paper proves (both are HOL results):
  - L follows from Ax2a and Ax2b using the satisfaction-operator witness $@_w\Pos$ (`L_R`). The detector flags that proof as hybrid.
  - L follows from the globally-positive set $\{\psi\mid \mathsf A(\Pos\psi)\}$ only under a proviso: positivity must be the same at every world (`L_R_rigidpos`).
- The paper says this proviso does not follow from the axioms (`RigidPos_of_link`, `pos_of_common_succ`).
- §Open questions (`sec:open`) leaves open the second question: does L follow under the rigid reading with no condition on positivity?
- K1 drops the proviso and claims validity over general models. That answers this open question for the PosGlobal instance. Prior art covers only the version with the proviso, or with a nominal.

**K2 (a separating v4 general model of Ax1Gen, Ax1GenTwo, Ax2a, Ax2b, Ax3, Ax4).**
- Source: same file, Example `ex:separating` in §`sec:criterion-open`.
- The paper builds a Fraenkel–Mostowski / Pitts-style $\mathcal L$-general model in which $\mathsf A\,\exists p.(\mathsf{Atom}\,p\wedge p)$ fails at every world. But that model satisfies only the **empty** set of sentences.
- The paper notes that this model is not a model of the variant-2 axioms, because modal collapse holds there hybrid-free.
- `sec:open` explicitly leaves open "whether a separating model exists for any of them" (the axioms).
- K2 is a separating model for a set of argument axioms. That is the open case, of which the paper's example is the trivial special case.
- Caution for the requester: Theorem `thm:gex` derives G_ex from Ax1Gen+Ax2a+Ax4, and Theorem `thm:consequences` derives MC in variant 2, both hybrid-free. A K2 model must therefore avoid variant 2's definitions of G/Ess/E, or else satisfy MC. Make sure that is consistent with K8.

**K3 / K4 (Ax1GenTwo with ∃x.⊤ etc. gives unrestricted Ax1Gen, in variant 2 / variant 3′, over general models).**
- Source: same file, Theorem `thm:plain` (Ax1GenTwoVariant3Plain) and the text after it in §`sec:plain`.
- The paper proves `Ax1Gen_of_Two` / `Two_equivalent` in variant 3′ through `OneWorld` and `R_Id`, which are nominal arguments. It says the same argument "goes through in variant 2, equivalence included (session E)".
- It states that "whether the equivalence holds hybrid-free is open". `sec:open` repeats that this is open for variants 2 and 3′.
- So the HOL (Henkin/standard-model) version of K3/K4 is prior art. The hybrid-free, $\mathcal L$-general-model version K3/K4 claims is listed as open.
- Proposition `prop:lgeneral` (soundness over $\mathcal L$-general models for proofs meeting the whole-term condition) is the tool that would turn a hybrid-free proof into the ValidV4 statement. It is not itself a proof of K1–K4.

The companion paper `sources/2609.26806/v3/main.tex` (Lean 4/THF port) defers all of these points to 2609.36279 (e.g. around lines 185–190 and 508). It contains nothing on general models, Ax1GenR or Ax1GenTwo beyond those references. No newer paper by Benzmüller or anyone else addressing these open questions turned up: the arXiv author listing shows only the two 2026 papers above.

## Related work
- C. Benzmüller, "Proofs Without Nominals …", arXiv 2609.36279 v4 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36279):
  - `L_R` (with a satisfaction operator) and `L_R_rigidpos` (with rigid positivity) are the conditional or hybrid versions of K1.
  - Example `ex:separating` is a separating $\mathcal L$-general model for the empty theory only. It is the special case of K2.
  - `Ax1Gen_of_Two` / `Two_equivalent` (variant 3′, and variant 2 per session E), proved with nominals, are the HOL versions of K3/K4.
  - `prop:lgeneral` is soundness over $\mathcal L$-general models.
  - `sec:open` lists the exact questions K1–K4 address as open.
- C. Benzmüller, "Gödel's and Scott's Variants … in Lean 4 and TPTP THF", arXiv 2609.26806 v3 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26806): the port the developments build on. It points to 2609.36279 for nominals and the open questions, and has no general-model results.
- C. Benzmüller, "A Comment on Modal Collapse and Ultrafilters", arXiv 2608.07578 (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.07578): rigidity of positivity as the driver of collapse. It is background for K1's proviso question and does not address general models.
