K1 says that under the rigid conjunction schema Ax1GenR, instantiated with the globally positive properties {ψ | Pψ = ⊤}, P G holds at every world of every v4 𝓛-general model (nonempty individuals) of variant-2 Ax2a, Ax2b and Ax4. It is backed by a certified deep derivation. I found no source that states or proves this. The nearest prior art is the primary source 2609.36279 v4, which proves weaker or neighbouring results and explicitly leaves this case open:

- `sources/2609.36279/v4/main.tex`, §"Ax1GenR: rigid sets" (`\label{sec:rigid}`). It defines Ax1GenR with Φ of type (e→σ)→o. Lemma `L_R` derives L (that is, P G) in K from Ax2a and Ax2b, but only with the hybrid witness @_w P, which the detector flags. `L_R_rigidpos` derives L from the set {ψ | A(Pψ)} (in their notation, Pψ = ⊤) using no axiom of the argument. It does so only under the proviso that positivity is the same set at every world. `RigidPos_of_link` shows that this proviso holds on weakly connected frames. The section says the proviso does not follow from the axioms. It records as open "whether L follows under this reading with no condition on positivity at all". The open-questions section (around lines 2155–2170) repeats this as the paper's second open question.
- The same file defines 𝓛-general models (`def:lgeneral`). Proposition `prop:lgeneral` gives soundness over them for HOL proofs that meet a whole-term condition, but that is a shallow-embedding argument and not a deep object-level derivation. The paper says its criterion "certifies that what a proof substitutes stays within the object language, not that the theorem has an object-level derivation" (§ around line 2085). It says a deep embedding is formalised only for the propositional part (`Separation.thy/.lean`, `ex:separating`).
- `sources/2609.26806/v3` (Lean/THF port) and the Notes: L is immediate from the world-dependent Ax1Gen with Φ = P (`lean.tex`, `theorem L`). That is the non-rigid reading, not Ax1GenR.

So K1 gives the Ax1GenR + {ψ | Pψ=⊤} result without the rigid-positivity proviso, using Ax2a/Ax2b/Ax4, with a deep derivation sound over 𝓛-general models. That appears to settle the open question recorded in 2609.36279 v4, and I found no publication that does. This is "not found in this search", not a proof of novelty. One caveat: K1 adds Ax4 and the nonempty-domain assumption. The open question in the source is stated for "this reading" generally. Whether K1 answers it exactly depends on those hypotheses being admissible in that question. As I read §sec:rigid, they are: Ax4 is an axiom of variant 2.
