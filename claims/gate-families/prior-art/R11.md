# Prior-art answer for R11 (K18: the prudent gate family G_{m,j})

Verdict: none found for the G_{m,j} family and its laws.

- No source defines G_{m,j} or another unexploitable agent that cooperates with FairBot and CooperateBot but defects against itself (see R3, R5, R6, R10).
- Related generalisations of PrudentBot, all different from G_{m,j}:
  - Barasz et al. 2014, remark after Theorem 3.2: the DefectBot check may use PA+n (informal graded PrudentBots; R4 answered partial).
  - LessWrong comments on Barasz et al.: indexed Prudent(m,n)Bot pairs (orthonormal, cousin_it).
  - Oesterheld 2022 (arXiv 2211.05057): PB_theta, a probabilistic PrudentBot generalisation indexed by a probability threshold, shown compatible with DUPOC (FairBot), CIMCIC and epsilon-grounded FairBot; all of these cooperate with themselves.
- No source compares an agent against ConBot (cooperate iff not box False) in this way.
