# Prior-art answer for R13 (K20: the H_{m,j} family)

Verdict: none found for the H_{m,j} family and its laws.

- No source defines H_{m,j} or any unexploitable agent that cooperates with FairBot and CooperateBot but defects against itself (see R3, R5, R6, R10, R11).
- Related generalisations of PrudentBot, all different and all self-cooperating: Barasz et al. remark after Theorem 3.2; LessWrong Prudent(m,n)Bot comments; Oesterheld 2022's probabilistic PB_theta.
- The safety argument (H implies box^{m+1} of the opponent's cooperation, so no modal opponent exploits it) follows the same pattern as Barasz et al.'s unexploitability results for FairBot-like agents; that pattern is known, its use for this family was not found.
