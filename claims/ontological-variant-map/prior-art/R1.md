# Prior-art answer for R1 (K2: A1, A2, A5 force isolation and collapse on symmetric frames; A4 unused)

Verdict: partial prior art.

- Known: Scott's T3 (necessary existence) and modal collapse need only symmetry of the accessibility relation (Benzmüller & Scott, Notes, Monatshefte 2025, and the AFP development; Benzmüller arXiv 2609.26806, Table 4). KB suffices (Benzmüller & Woltzenlogel Paleo).
- Known: A4 is not needed for some steps: in Gödel's variant 2, Th4 does not need Ax4 (arXiv 2609.26806); with rigid positivity A4 holds trivially (drewarrowood/godel-ontological).
- Related: uniqueness of essences (an essence of x is necessarily had only by x; UniqueEss lemmas in the AFP development), and in Gödel's variant 3 an essence of x can only be "being x" (arXiv 2609.36279). This is close to the haecceity argument but not the same statement.
- Not found: the statement that A1, A2 and A5 alone (without A3 and A4), with Scott essence and full intensional properties, force R w v ↔ v = w on symmetric frames via a haecceity essence; or the euclidean-frame necessary-existence result.
- Suggested wording: the symmetry dependence and partial A4 savings are known (Benzmüller & Scott; Benzmüller 2026); the isolation result from A1, A2, A5 alone and the haecceity route were not found.
