# Prior-art answer for R8 (K9: Anderson's variant, minimal axioms and frame table; A5 derivable)

Verdict: partial prior art (substantial).

- Known: redundancy of axioms in Anderson's variant is a documented controversy. Magari and Hájek claimed A4 and A5 are superfluous/redundant; Anderson & Gettings ("Gödel's Ontological Proof Revisited", Gödel '96) argued redundancy holds only with constant domains. Benzmüller, Weber & Woltzenlogel Paleo, "Computer-Assisted Analysis of the Anderson–Hájek Ontological Controversy" (Logica Universalis 2017) show A5 (positivity of necessary existence) redundant already in K and A4 redundant in K4B, under constant and varying domains, and that Anderson's emendation does not entail collapse. Bjørdal's alternative gives T3 in KB from a small axiom set.
- Overlap with K9: "A5 is derivable from A2 and H" and "no collapse" are known.
- Not found: the full Anderson table over subsets × nine frame classes, the result that A1a, A2 and P(G_A) alone give existence and necessary existence exactly on B, KB, S5 (and necessary existence also on KD45), and the KD45 boundary where actual existence needs A4.
- Suggested wording: A5's redundancy and non-collapse are known (Hájek; Benzmüller, Weber & Woltzenlogel Paleo 2017); the minimal set and the frame-class table, including the KD45/A4 boundary, were not found.
