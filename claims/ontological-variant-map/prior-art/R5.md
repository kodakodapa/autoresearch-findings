# Prior-art answer for R5 (K7: rigid-property table; no collapse, existence from A1,A2,A3 or A1,A3,A5 on every frame)

Verdict: partial prior art.

- Known: avoiding collapse by restricting the properties quantified over: Fitting's extensional variant (Types, Tableaus and Gödel's God, 2002) and Anderson's variant avoid collapse (also Benzmüller & Fuenmayor, arXiv 1910.08955, ultrafilters distinguishing intensional and extensional positive properties). A Lean development (drewarrowood/godel-ontological) treats rigid positivity: A4 becomes free and a countermodel to global T3 exists.
- Not found: the complete rigid-property table over 32 subsets × Scott/Gödel essence × nine frame classes, the result that existence holds on every frame exactly for supersets of {A1,A2,A3} or (Scott essence) {A1,A3,A5}, that A4 is never needed there, and that no consistent cell forces collapse.
- Suggested wording: collapse-avoidance by restricting properties is known (Fitting; Anderson; Benzmüller & Fuenmayor); the rigid-property table and its minimal sets were not found.

## Related work
- the single cells and per-proof axiom dependencies of Scott's and Gödel's variants established with other provers (Benzmüller & Scott 2025; Benzmüller 2026)
- the analysis of Anderson's variant and the redundancy of its axioms (Benzmüller, Weber & Woltzenlogel Paleo 2017)
- Fitting's and Anderson's collapse-free variants (Fitting 2002; Benzmüller & Fuenmayor 2019)
- Benzmüller's simplified variant (2020)
