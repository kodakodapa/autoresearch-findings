Claim K14: in session `Ax1GenRigidVariant2` (logic K, rigid reading `Ax1GenR`) of arXiv 2609.36279 v4, `L_R` (Ax1GenR → ⌊P G⌋) has a proof that uses the object-language witness `PosGlobal` (A(P ψ)), uses only Ax2a, Ax2b and Ax4, and puts no condition on positivity.

No source I found states or proves this. The closest source is the v4 paper, and it records the matter as open:

- `sources/2609.36279/v4/main.tex`, §"Ax1GenR: rigid sets" (`\label{sec:rigid}`). L follows in K from Ax2a and Ax2b only with the satisfaction-operator witness @_w P (`L_R`). With the witness {ψ | A(P ψ)}, L follows only if positivity is the same at every world (`L_R_rigidpos`). The section ends by naming as open whether L follows under this reading with no condition on positivity at all. It adds that both world-free descriptions of positivity need that condition, and that Φ := {G} turns the premise of the axiom into its own conclusion.
- `sources/2609.36279/v4/anc/OpenQuestions/lean/OpenQuestions/Ax1GenRigidVariant2.lean`:
  - `L_R` uses the witness `fun ψ => P ψ w`.
  - `L_R_rigidpos` uses `PosGlobal` but needs the hypothesis `hr : RigidPos`.
  - `RigidPos` is stated as not following from the axioms, with or without `Ax1GenR`. Both are `countermodel` examples.
  - `RigidPos_of_link` gives the condition only on weakly connected frames.
  - The module comment says outright that whether L follows without such a condition is open.
- `sources/2609.36279/v4/anc/OpenQuestions/audit/census-openquestions.txt` lists `PosGlobal` as a witness only for `L_R_rigidpos`. It never pairs `PosGlobal` with `L_R`.

So v4 has a weaker result: the `PosGlobal` witness under the extra hypothesis RigidPos. Separately, it has the full statement with a world-naming witness. It also says plainly that the unconditional, object-language version is open. The claimed proof would therefore settle a question v4 leaves open. It would also need some way around the paper's remark that both world-free descriptions need RigidPos, perhaps through Ax4. No paper I found gives such a proof.

The companion paper 2609.26806 v3 has no `Ax1GenR` session, and I found nothing on the web that covers the claim. This is a "not found" result. It does not show that the claim is true or that it is new.

One side note from reading the sources: they disagree about the detector on `L_R`. `main.tex` §sec:rigid says the detector flags the @_w P witness. The Lean module comment says the detector "does not flag it, and cannot", because the witness's type is not world-lifted. This affects the "v4's detector passes" part of the claim and should be checked against `anc/OpenQuestions/audit/report.txt` (entry `L_R`).
