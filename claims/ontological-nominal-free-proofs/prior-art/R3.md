Basis: source

Every statement in K3–K8 and K12 is already proved in v4 of arXiv 2609.36279 ("Proofs Without Nominals"), under the same theorem names and in the same Lean modules (`sources/2609.36279/v4/anc/OpenQuestions/lean/OpenQuestions/*.lean`). But v4's own detector does **not** pass those proofs. The audit file `sources/2609.36279/v4/anc/OpenQuestions/audit/report.txt` lists them as having flagged witnesses or as inheriting hybridness:
- Ax1GenBoxVariant3: `Ax1Gen_of_refl`, `MC_S4` and `Th5_S4` inherit from `OneWorld`/`NoNecExist`, whose witnesses are flagged NOMINAL RIGID.
- Ax1GenOneVariant3: `AllActual_of_PBot` is flagged ACTUAL, `AllExist_One` NOMINAL. `MC_unconditional`, `MC_One`, `E_top_of_PBot`, `NotPBot/PTop/G_ex/Th4_One_deriv`, `One_gives_S`, `One_gives_Box` and `Box_gives_One` inherit.
- Ax1GenTwoVariant3: `MC_unconditional`, `MC_Two`, `L_Two_hybrid`, `G_ex_Two_actual_hybrid` and `Th4_Two_actual_hybrid` inherit, through `OneWorld`, `EssPt`, `PtNe` and `E_only_here`.
- Ax1GenTwoVariant3PlainPoss and Ax1GenTwoVariant3PlainAndersonQuant: `MC_unconditional` inherits.
- Ax1GenTwoVariant3Th4: `AllExist_Two` is flagged ACTUAL, and `G_ex_Two_hybrid` inherits.
- Ax1GenTwoVariant3Plain: `AllExist` is flagged ACTUAL, and `MC_OneWorld` inherits.

The paper says the same in its text:
- §sec:v3, Theorem thm:v3 (`thm:v3`): OneWorld and MC go through the nominal of Theorem `thm:nonecexist`.
- §sec:box: `Ax1Gen_of_refl`, `Th5_S4` and `MC_S4` take on the nominals of `OneWorld`.
- §sec:one: for variant 3 under Ax1GenOne, the paper says the hybrid-free question remains open.
- Theorem `thm:v3-two`(c): `G_ex_Two_hybrid` uses nominals.
- §sec:open lists the related open questions. These include whether condition (†) can be dropped hybrid-free, and whether the Notes' results hold hybrid-free under the one-conjunct reading.

So v4 has the statements but not detector-passing proofs of them. I found no other source that gives such proofs, in particular no hybrid-free proof that every σ is constant or that there is only one world. One discrepancy inside v4 should be noted. Theorem `thm:v3`(c) and Theorem `thm:plain`(d) call `AllExist` hybrid-free, but the Lean audit flags its witness `fun z => z =ᵐ y ∧ᵐ ¬ᵐ existsAt z` as ACTUAL. In that one respect, K8/K12's `AllExist` claims may overlap with what v4 already asserts in its text.

K11 is a separating countermodel to NoNecExist: two worlds, permutation-invariant domains, and E holding everywhere. v4 has no such model. Its only Lean model, `Ax1GenTwoVariant3Model.lean`, has a single world (Unit), and in it E is empty. The closest related work is Example `ex:separating` in §sec:criterion-open (main.tex) and `anc/OpenQuestions/lean/Separation.lean`. That example builds an L-general model from invariance under flips (a Fraenkel–Mostowski construction) and separates a sentence the embedding proves only with a nominal. It does so for the *empty* theory, though. §sec:open explicitly leaves open "whether a separating model exists for any of" the article's axioms. K11's method is therefore anticipated, but its specific result is not.

The web searches found no outside work, including the community Lean repositories and the 2609.26806 port, that addresses detector-certified or nominal-free proofs of these variant-3 statements.

## Related work
- C. Benzmüller, "Proofs Without Nominals", arXiv 2609.36279 v4 (sources/2609.36279/v4): proves every statement in K3–K8 and K12 under the same names, but with nominal or actuality witnesses that its detector flags (audit/report.txt). It states that the hybrid-free versions are open for the Ax1GenOne variant 3 and for dropping (†) (§sec:one, §sec:open). Example ex:separating and Separation.lean give a flip-invariant separating model for the empty theory, which is the same technique K11 uses, applied to a different theory.

- alozanoroble/godel-ontological-lean and drewarrowood/godel-ontological (GitHub): Lean formalisations of the variants. Neither addresses nominal-free proofs.
