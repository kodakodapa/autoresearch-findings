# Prior-art answer for R32 (K58 again: depth bound for separating F_k from F_{k+1})

Verdict: partial (same as R30).

- This is the same claim as R30. Its back-and-forth method is the standard n-bisimulation technique (Blackburn, de Rijke & Venema, Modal Logic, 2001); its application to graded FairBots was not found. See prior-art-1600/R30.md.
